# Rotational imbalance and bent-shaft reference

## Scope

This card provides general context for the distinct SentinelAI `imbalance` and
`bent_shaft` labels.

## What this evidence can mean

Fluke describes rotational unbalance as a mass distribution whose inertia axis does not
coincide with the rotation axis. A bent shaft can contribute to an unbalance-like
response, and its vibration indications can overlap those associated with imbalance or
angular misalignment. Signal overlap means corroboration matters.

## Common inspection considerations

Compare vibration with a stable baseline and operating state. Depending on equipment and
site procedure, inspect for missing or shifted mass, deposits, uneven wear, assembly
changes, shaft runout, phase behavior, and coupling alignment.

## What this evidence does not establish

The model result does not by itself locate a heavy spot, prove a shaft is bent, quantify
runout, measure fault severity, or prescribe balancing or shaft replacement.
