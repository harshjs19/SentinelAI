# Shaft alignment and coupling reference

## Scope

This card supports the SentinelAI `misalignment` and `coupling_fault` labels for coupled
rotating equipment. The two runtime labels remain distinct.

## What this evidence can mean

The U.S. Department of Energy motor-system sourcebook describes angular, parallel, and
combined shaft misalignment. It notes that excessive misalignment can produce vibration,
noise, coupling or bearing temperature changes, and loads that shorten component life.
Flexible couplings tolerate only the limits declared by their manufacturer; flexibility
is not a substitute for alignment.

## Common inspection considerations

Check shaft alignment using the equipment and coupling manufacturer's tolerances. Review
coupling condition, fasteners, foundations, soft-foot or settling indications, and
vibration trends where site procedures support those checks.

## What this evidence does not establish

A classifier label alone does not locate the alignment error, prove coupling damage,
measure severity, determine urgency, or authorize shutdown or repair.

## Source basis

SentinelAI-authored paraphrase of the U.S. Department of Energy, *Improving Motor and
Drive System Performance: A Sourcebook for Industry*, including its shaft-alignment tip
sheet. No sourcebook section is reproduced.
