# Bearing fault reference

## Scope

This card provides general interpretation and inspection context for a SentinelAI
`bearing_fault` finding. It does not identify a bearing failure mode or root cause.

## What this evidence can mean

Rolling-bearing damage can arise through different mechanisms and may present through
changes such as vibration, noise, temperature, or visible surface damage. SKF's damage
analysis guidance stresses that observed damage should be interpreted together with
operating conditions, speed, load, lubrication information, and the assembly design.

## Common inspection considerations

Preserve operating context and examine the bearing, lubricant, fits, seating, adjacent
components, loading, and alignment as applicable to the equipment and site procedure.
Corroborating condition measurements can help distinguish a repeatable change from an
isolated indication.

## What this evidence does not establish

The finding is not a specific ISO 15243 failure-mode classification, remaining-life
estimate, fault severity, failure probability, or instruction to replace a bearing.

## Source basis

SentinelAI-authored paraphrase of SKF, *Bearing damage and failure analysis*. No SKF
manual text or tables are redistributed.
