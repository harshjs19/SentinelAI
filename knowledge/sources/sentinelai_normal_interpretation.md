# Normal-condition interpretation

## Scope

This card explains a SentinelAI `healthy` finding or `normal` Analysis condition.

## What this evidence can mean

Within the producing model's declared labels, threshold, validated scope, and input, the
result mapped to the normal condition. The Evidence Package preserves the exact model
scope and any Analysis limitations needed to qualify that statement.

## Common inspection considerations

Interpret the result alongside the producing model's lifecycle status, its validated
scope, data quality, operating context, and any established site monitoring process.

## What this evidence does not establish

Normal does not prove that the machine has no defects. It does not exclude unmodeled
faults, validate use on a new asset population, create a health score, or warrant a
change to scheduled inspection.
