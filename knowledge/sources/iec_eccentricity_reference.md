# Induction-motor eccentricity reference

## Scope

This card provides bounded context for a SentinelAI `eccentric_rotor` finding in rotating
electrical machinery.

## What this evidence can mean

IEC TS 60034-24 lists static and dynamic eccentricities among potential active-part
failures addressed by online detection and diagnosis guidance for rotating electrical
machines. Eccentricity concerns non-uniformity in the rotor-stator air gap; determining
its form and cause requires machine-specific evidence.

## Common inspection considerations

Use the motor manufacturer's tolerances and qualified procedures when reviewing air-gap
condition, rotor position, bearings, shaft condition, alignment, mounting, and relevant
electrical or vibration signatures. Compare evidence across operating conditions where
the method requires it.

## What this evidence does not establish

The finding does not identify static versus dynamic eccentricity, a root cause, clearance,
severity, remaining life, or an operational decision.
