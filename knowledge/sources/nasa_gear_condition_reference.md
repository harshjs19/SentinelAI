# Gear-wear condition reference

## Scope

This card supports the separate SentinelAI labels `gear_wear_25`, `gear_wear_50`, and
`gear_wear_75`. Their numbers are dataset class names, not a Retriever measurement of
physical wear percentage or severity.

## What this evidence can mean

NASA gear-condition experiments show that gear damage progression may be investigated
with complementary evidence such as vibration, oil-debris measurements, and documented
visual inspection. The reported diagnostic indicators were influenced by operating
speed and load, so a signal must be interpreted in its experimental and machine context.

## Common inspection considerations

Under the equipment's approved maintenance procedure, preserve load and speed context;
review lubrication and debris evidence; inspect accessible tooth surfaces and contact
patterns; and compare vibration or noise with an appropriate baseline.

## What this evidence does not establish

The SentinelAI class does not quantify damaged tooth area, assign severity, predict
failure, or prescribe repair. NASA test-rig results do not validate the CORA classifier.
